HW 9 — SwiftUI UI prototype (frontend only)

This milestone moves you toward the full end-to-end Project 2. For this assignment, focus on the frontend only—no backend or full CRUD required yet.

Build a rough SwiftUI prototype of your app. You do not need every screen finished, but your project should show:

Navigation into most of the main UI areas you plan for the final app
The app builds and runs on both iPhone and iPad (use appropriate layouts or size classes where needed)
What to submit

Upload two items:

A ZIP of your Xcode/SwiftUI project (everything needed to open and run it)

A PDF and/or a Loom video that shows screenshots or screen recordings of the main UI in both:

iPhone configuration
iPad configuration
Label or call out the main features in your PDF or video so it is easy to see what you have wired up so far.
