# Crit without miss

Wiki documents both crit and miss as low chance. For class MVP we use **20% crit** (`invented`): crit ignores evasion and deals firepower damage; **does not pierce Protect**. **Luck** raises crit to **35%**. **No separate miss roll** — “soldier vs AT-AT” style failures are modeled by evasion (and Protect/shields), and miss RNG felt lame for matchups like X-Wing vs infantry.
