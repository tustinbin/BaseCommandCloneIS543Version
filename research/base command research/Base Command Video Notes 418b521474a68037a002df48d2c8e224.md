# Base Command Video Notes

### HOW TO PLAY BASE COMMAND

BASE COMMAND: In base command, you defend your base from invading forces. Defeat all enemies to complete the Siege. Use your star cards from Battlefront to customize and improve your deck, and earn Credits by getting enough score to earn Stars

STAR CARDS & UNIT CARDS: Use your Star Cards from Battlefront to trigger powerful events and boost your Unit Cards. Some Star Cards are played on your base or target enemy units, while others are attached to your unit cards.

YOUR BASE: This is your base. If it reaches 0 health, the game is over. It is protected by shields that go down as enemies attack them. Your base cannon increases in firepower at the start of each turn.

DISCARDING: At the start of each round you can discard a number of cards equal to your number of available discard points. You earn 1 new discard point every round. you can have 2 on hold at once. start game with 1

SCORE & CREDITS: Earn score by winning in combat and by completing a siege with plenty of base health, shields, and ranked-up units. If you get enough score, you earn Stars which in turn earn you Credits

# Units

Units have base stats and different perks

## Universal Unit Stats

- Attacks (number of attacks they do in battle before disengaging)
- Agility (you subtract this from the opposing units firepower to see how much damage is dealt. higher agility gives you a chance to dodge attacks)
- Shields (depleted before hp, one shield blocks one full attack)
- Firepower (damage to deal)
- Health (damage to take)

## Perks

- Speed boost (enemy perk): moves 2 steps per turn
- squad leader (player perk): draw a new unit card after unit finishes combat
- first strike (both sides perk): will attack first in combat. if enemy and player have it then player goes first (player goes first by default if no one has first strike)
- cloaked (enemy perk): spawns in not on the outer ring (second ring usually)
- protected (enemy perk): they ignore the first attack that would deal damage to them
- overload (both sides perk): when engaging in combat, it will use all of its attacks in the first round of combat

# Random facts

- when an enemy unit runs into the base shield it breaks the shield and deals 1 damage to the enemy
- during each turn you start out with 3 star cards and 3 unit cards drawn from your deck. if you use two units you will get two units to replace those slots next turn
- Click a unit then hover an enemy and it will flash their health bar how much either is expected to lose when exchanging damage for each of their number of attacks but that doesn’t take critical hits or dodges into consideration
- after you use a card it goes in the discard. if it was a unit and tied or lost the battle nothing happened, if it won it gets a stat rank up.
- star cards that attach to units are discarded when that unit is discarded
- when all unit cards are in their discard pile or all star cards are in their discard pile they are mixed and put back in the draw pile
- enemy units with shields replenish one shield each round
- base cannon damage increases for each turn of not using it from 1 to 3 to 5
- you have a score multiplier that increases by 1x every 4 enemies killed but if one of your units dies it decreases by 1x
- loading in to a siege it tells you the enemy units present in the level and how many credits you earn at each star achieved

# Star cards

- in the select menu the star cards are grouped by type and you can only have a certain number of them in your deck at a time
- hero star cards are units but go in your star card deck (not your unit deck)

    Base Command is a mini-game linked with [Star Wars Battlefront](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%EC%8A%A4%ED%83%80%EC%9B%8C%EC%A6%88%20%EB%B0%B0%ED%8B%80%ED%94%84%EB%A1%A0%ED%8A%B8) . You can play it after logging in on [the Battlefront website](http://starwars.ea.com/starwars/battlefront/basecommand) , or you can play it with the dedicated [application](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%EC%95%A0%ED%94%8C%EB%A6%AC%EC%BC%80%EC%9D%B4%EC%85%98) Companion App.It is a defense-type game using a card system. Most cards can be used that have been purchased/unlocked in the original game, and some cards can be unlocked by meeting specific conditions within the original game. Additionally, credits earned from base commands are linked to the original game. Since most of the cards unlocked in the game have good performance, it is best to actively unlock them to ensure smooth progress and high scores.
    
    ## [2.](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%EC%8A%A4%ED%83%80%EC%9B%8C%EC%A6%88%20%EB%B0%B0%ED%8B%80%ED%94%84%EB%A1%A0%ED%8A%B8/%EB%B2%A0%EC%9D%B4%EC%8A%A4%20%EC%BB%A4%EB%A7%A8%EB%93%9C#toc) system
    
    The player becomes the commander of a [Rebel Alliance](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%EB%B0%98%EB%9E%80%20%EC%97%B0%ED%95%A9) base and must fend off [Galactic Empire](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%EC%9D%80%ED%95%98%20%EC%A0%9C%EA%B5%AD(%EC%8A%A4%ED%83%80%EC%9B%8C%EC%A6%88)) units coming to destroy the base . Imperial units spawn at the edge of the board and move toward the center, reaching the base in the center and taking damage, causing the base to run out of health and destroy it, resulting in game over.
    
    - ★ is given according to the resulting score, and you can get a minimum ★★★ maximum ★★★ rating. You receive credit according to your grade.
    - Each time you kill an enemy, the score multiplier meter increases by 1/4. If you kill 4 enemies, the score multiplier increases to 2, and if you kill 8 enemies, the score multiplier increases to 3. If a friendly unit is defeated, its multiplier is immediately reduced by one level (not 1/4!). If you want to aim for ★★★, you must take this into consideration.

    - This disk-shaped terrain, which is the stage of the game, is called **a board** . At the center of the board is the base, and enemy units spawn at the very edge of the board unless stealth perks are used, approaching the interior one space per turn.
    - The distance from the center to the outside is called **the range** . Ranges range from 1 to 4, with range 0 being the base.
    - The area that divides the board into the upper left, upper right, lower left, and lower right quarters is called **a Sector** . All enemy units do not move out of their zones, except for the hero units Boba, Vader, and Palpatine, which can change zones.
    - One range of one section of the board, that is, one square, the minimum unit, is called **a coordinate** . Up to two units can coexist per coordinate. There are a total of 16 coordinates on the board.

    A base that the player must protect. The base is simply composed of a shield generator + ATGAR 1.4 FD P-Tower.
    
    - The base's health is 10, and it does not recover automatically unless a card is used. The remaining health at the end of the siege is reflected in the score. When an enemy reaches the base with a range of 0 and penetrates the base, it deals damage and disappears. The damage inflicted at this time is equal to the firepower of the unit.
    - There are a total of four **shields** , one in each direction. When an enemy unit reaches range 1 and attacks the shield, the shield disappears and the enemy takes 1 damage. Unless a card is used, the lost shield will not be restored. The number of shields remaining at the end of the siege is reflected in the score.
    
    When using a squad shield or using a base shield card on an existing shield, the shield turns purple and becomes overcharged. The overcharged shield is not destroyed even if the enemy attacks, and the attacked enemy takes 2 damage. The overcharge status disappears at the start of the next turn.Enemies with the Ion Weapon perk ignore the shield and go straight into the base, so be careful.
    
    - The 1.4 FD P-Tower, also known as **the Base Cannon** , can attack one enemy unit within a range of 3. Attacking consumes energy, which recharges at the start of each turn and is fully consumed after a single shot. Therefore, it can only attack once per turn. The damage dealt is equal to the accumulated energy and ignores enemy evasion. If the energy reaches 0 after being used once, it recharges by 1 at the start of the next turn, and then increases by 2 each subsequent turn, progressing from 1 > 3 > 5. It will not recharge beyond 5.
    
    The Base Cannon should be used very strategically, as it is better to have your allies take the last hit of the enemy and aim for a special advance. It is good to charge it all the way to the end and shoot a powerful 5-point beam, but it is also good to remove the protection of enemies with protection perks with a 1-point beam.When using the Anti-Material Turret card, the damage is doubled only once, so if you fill it up to 5 and shoot it, any unit can be destroyed in one hit due to the nature of ignoring the enemy's evasion value. However, be careful because you cannot ignore protective Perc.
    
    ### [2.3.](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%EC%8A%A4%ED%83%80%EC%9B%8C%EC%A6%88%20%EB%B0%B0%ED%8B%80%ED%94%84%EB%A1%A0%ED%8A%B8/%EB%B2%A0%EC%9D%B4%EC%8A%A4%20%EC%BB%A4%EB%A7%A8%EB%93%9C#toc) card use
    
    The cards are divided into **star cards** on the left and **unit cards** on the right .
    
    - There are many different types of **star cards** , and they can strengthen friendly units, strengthen the base, or attack enemies directly. Star cards allow players to designate 24 cards, and in addition to the starter deck for beginners, there are two customization deck slots. In some special sieges , there is a condition that only designated decks be used with configurations that are generally impossible .
    - **Unit cards** are Rebel Alliance units capable of attacking enemies, and are divided into Soldiers, [Snowspeeders](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%EC%8A%A4%EB%85%B8%EC%9A%B0%EC%8A%A4%ED%94%BC%EB%8D%94) , [A-wings](https://en.namu.wiki/w/A-wing) , and [X-wings](https://en.namu.wiki/w/X-wing) . Since the unit composition is fixed for each siege, it is impossible for the player to arbitrarily select the card composition.
    
    At the start of the player's turn, 3 star cards and 3 unit cards are given, and unused cards are kept until the next turn. In other words, except for special cases, a maximum of 6 cards can be used in one turn.If you wish to exchange a card for another, you can do so by spending **a Discard Point** located in the top right corner . You are given one Discard Point at the start of each turn, and you can hold a maximum of two. Therefore, the maximum number of cards you can exchange at one time is two. Since you cannot know which card will appear, you may receive an even more useless card if you are unlucky. Card exchanges are only possible after the turn begins and before using a card or attacking an enemy with a Base Cannon, etc.In the case of unit cards, the number of cards is set, and when they are used up, they are rotated to the first unit. When the player's unit kills an enemy unit , it becomes **special** and can grow from rank 0 to 3. Each time your rank goes up, your abilities are strengthened, and conversely, if you engage an enemy and are defeated, your rank is reset again.
    
    ### [2.4.](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%EC%8A%A4%ED%83%80%EC%9B%8C%EC%A6%88%20%EB%B0%B0%ED%8B%80%ED%94%84%EB%A1%A0%ED%8A%B8/%EB%B2%A0%EC%9D%B4%EC%8A%A4%20%EC%BB%A4%EB%A7%A8%EB%93%9C#toc) battle
    
    All units have 4 stats.
    
    | **attack** | Number of attacks during engagement. |
    | --- | --- |
    | **fire power** | The amount of damage inflicted on an enemy with one attack. |
    | **evasion** | A value that neutralizes enemy attacks. In reality, it is closer to 'defence'. |
    | **health** | Literally bloody. |
    
    If you select an allied unit and order it to attack an enemy, the allied unit will attack first. They will exchange attacks once each for a maximum of 'Attack' counts, dealing damage equal to <Allied Unit's Firepower - Enemy Unit's Evasion> per attack. In other words, a rebel soldier with 3 firepower will miss every time they attack an AT-AT with 4 evasion.
    
    There is a low chance of
    
    **a critical hit**
    
    occurring during an attack. When a critical hit occurs, not only is the damage increased, but it also ignores the enemy's evasion stat. In other words, if a critical hit occurs, even a Rebel soldier can inflict damage on an AT-AT. Additionally, based on a similar principle, there is a low chance of a Miss occurring regardless of evasion or firepower stats.
