# Video evidence log

Primary evidence for the game bible. One row per observation. Promote confirmed facts into `bible/` with confidence `observed` and the **Log ID** below.

## How to fill

1. Open a YouTube (or local) Base Command clip  
2. Pause on readable UI / combat resolution  
3. Add a row — be specific about what you *saw*, not what you assume  
4. Update `bible/star-cards.md`, `units.md`, `sieges.md`, or `GAME_BIBLE.md`
